Our purpose

Good food. Good soil. Good neighbours.

resQthali is an India-wide food rescue network built around one simple idea: surplus should be shared before it is discarded. We connect verified restaurants, volunteers, and trusted NGOs so food reaches people quickly and respectfully.

When food is no longer safe to serve, our responsible waste pathway turns it into vermicompost. We deliver that compost to farmers at the lowest possible price, helping improve soil health without making farming more expensive.

A careful note on the numbers

There is no reliable public figure for “how many people die of hunger” in India each year. Hunger is tracked through undernourishment, food insecurity, child nutrition, and mortality indicators rather than one single count. Source: 2025 Global Hunger Index. Globally, FAO estimates 645 million people may have faced hunger in 2025.
